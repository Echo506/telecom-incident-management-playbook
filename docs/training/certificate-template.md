## How to Use This Template

### Option 1: Print and Sign (Physical Certificate)

1. Copy the certificate template above.
2. Paste into a Word document or Google Doc.
3. Replace `[STUDENT NAME]` and `[DATE]` with actual values.
4. Add your company logo at the bottom (optional).
5. Print on certificate paper or cardstock.
6. Have instructor/manager sign and date.
7. Present to student upon course completion.

### Option 2: Digital Certificate (PDF)

1. Copy the certificate template above.
2. Use a design tool (Canva, Adobe, PowerPoint) to create a visually appealing certificate.
3. Add company branding, colors, and logo.
4. Export as PDF.
5. Email to student or share via learning management system.

### Option 3: Automated Certificate (Advanced)

If you have a learning management system (LMS) or can build a simple web app:

1. Create a form where students enter their name.
2. Use a PDF generation library (e.g., jsPDF, ReportLab) to auto-generate certificates.
3. Email certificate automatically upon course completion.
4. Store certificates in a shared drive or cloud storage for record-keeping.
