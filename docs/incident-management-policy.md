# Incident Management Policy

## Purpose

This policy establishes a consistent approach for identifying, logging, prioritizing, investigating, resolving, documenting, and reviewing service incidents.

The primary objective is to restore normal service operation as quickly as possible while minimizing business impact and maintaining accurate communication with affected stakeholders.

## Scope

This policy applies to incidents involving:

- Network connectivity and circuit outages
- Routers, switches, firewalls, and managed CPE
- Internet, SD-WAN, VPN, and cloud connectivity services
- Carrier, vendor, and third-party service failures
- Monitoring alerts and customer-reported service issues
- Security-related events that affect availability, confidentiality, or integrity

## Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Incident | An unplanned interruption to a service or reduction in service quality |
| Major Incident | A high-impact incident requiring urgent coordination, escalation, and frequent communication |
| Problem | The underlying cause of one or more incidents |
| Change | An addition, modification, or removal that may affect IT services |
| SLA | A service-level agreement defining expected response and resolution targets |
| Runbook | A documented procedure for performing repeatable operational tasks |

## Policy Requirements

- All incidents must be recorded in the approved ticketing system.
- Tickets must include accurate timestamps, affected service details, symptoms, actions taken, and communications.
- Incidents must be prioritized using impact and urgency.
- Major incidents require an incident owner and defined customer-update cadence.
- Carrier and vendor escalations must include relevant technical evidence and ticket references.
- Service restoration must be validated before closure.
- Major or recurring incidents require a post-incident review.
- Documentation and runbooks must be updated when lessons learned identify process gaps.

## Incident Status Flow

1. New
2. Acknowledged
3. In Progress
4. Pending Customer, Vendor, or Carrier
5. Resolved
6. Closed

## Review Cycle

This policy should be reviewed at least annually and after major incidents, material service changes, or significant regulatory changes.
