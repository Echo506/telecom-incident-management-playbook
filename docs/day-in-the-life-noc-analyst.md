# Day in the Life of a NOC Analyst

## Overview

This document describes typical activities, responsibilities, and skills for a NOC analyst in a telecommunications and managed services environment.

## Typical Shift Activities

- Monitor dashboards and alarm queues for new incidents.
- Triage alerts: validate, classify, and prioritize.
- Create and update incident tickets with accurate chronology.
- Perform initial troubleshooting: reachability tests, device status, recent changes.
- Escalate to carriers, vendors, or internal Tier 2/3 teams.
- Communicate with customers via email, chat, or phone.
- Follow up on pending carrier or vendor tickets.
- Document resolution steps and closure notes.
- Participate in handovers and shift briefings.

## Key Skills

- Network fundamentals (routing, switching, firewalls, access circuits)
- Ticketing and ITSM tools (Salesforce, ServiceNow, Jira Service Management)
- Monitoring platforms (SolarWinds, PRTG, custom AIOps)
- Clear, professional written communication
- Time management and prioritization under SLA pressure
- Basic scripting or automation (optional but valuable)

## Common Incident Types

- Circuit outages (fiber, Ethernet, broadband)
- Device unreachable (router, switch, firewall, CPE)
- High latency and packet loss
- DNS, DHCP, or routing issues
- Power or environmental issues at remote sites
- Security-related events (DDoS, compromised devices)

## Career Progression

- NOC Tier 1 → Tier 2 → Tier 3
- Major Incident Manager
- Network Engineer / Security Analyst
- ITSM Process Owner / GRC Analyst
