# Customer Initial Acknowledgment Template

## Purpose

Provide a consistent, professional initial notification when an incident is created.

## Template (English)

**Subject:** Ticket [{{CASE_ID}}] – {{ISSUE_TYPE}} – {{SITE_NAME}}

Hello {{CUSTOMER_NAME}},

We have created support case **{{CASE_ID}}** for a **{{ISSUE_TYPE}}** affecting your site at **{{SITE_ADDRESS}}**.

**Initial trouble reported:** {{SYMPTOMS}}  
**Incident start time:** {{INCIDENT_START_TIME}}  
**Affected service:** {{SERVICE_DESCRIPTION}}

Our team is currently investigating and will provide an update by {{NEXT_UPDATE_TIME}} or sooner if significant information becomes available.

If you observe any additional symptoms or have further details, please reply to this email.

Best regards,  
{{TECH_NAME}} / NOC Team  
{{COMPANY_NAME}}  
{{CONTACT_INFO}}
